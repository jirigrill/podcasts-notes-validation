# 07 — MRR arithmetic: what funnel does $2–5k MRR actually require?

Type: research
Status: open
Blocked by: 03, 04, 05, 06

## Question

Build the **explicit arithmetic** from the $2–5k MRR success bar back to the installs and traffic
required, then judge reachability for a solo developer with no audience.

Depends on 03 and 05 for competitor price points, 04 for audience size, 06 for the cost side.

Answer specifically:

- **Plausible price points**: anchored to Snipd's and Airr's actual pricing (from 03/05), plus
  Readwise as the upper anchor for PKM willingness-to-pay. Consider monthly, annual, and
  lifetime. State the price you'd actually pick and why.
- **Conversion rates for indie iOS subscription apps**: find real benchmark data — RevenueCat's
  *State of Subscription Apps* report is the best public source; also Adapty and Appfigures
  data. Get: install→trial, trial→paid, and monthly churn for the productivity category, and the
  **median** rather than the headline figures (the median indie app earns far less than the mean).
- Then compute, showing every step:
  - paying users required at each price point for $2k and $5k MRR
  - installs required, given install→paid conversion
  - App Store impressions / traffic required, given typical page-conversion
  - and the **churn treadmill**: monthly new paying users needed just to hold steady.
- **Subtract the cost side** from 06 to show net margin and the real Apple-cut-adjusted revenue.
- **Time to get there**: given a cold start with no audience, how long plausibly? Look for
  evidence from indie developers who have published revenue timelines for comparable apps.
- **Judge reachability plainly.** Include the base rate: what share of indie iOS subscription apps
  ever reach $2k MRR? RevenueCat publishes distribution data on this — use it. This base rate is
  probably the single most decision-relevant number in the whole map, so find it if it exists.

Distinguish verified benchmark data from inferred assumptions. Label every assumption explicitly
so the developer can challenge each one independently.
