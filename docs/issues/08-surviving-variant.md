# 08 — Which concept variant survives the constraints?

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 01, 02, 03, 05

## Question

A decision ticket, worked live with the developer. By this point the constraints are known:
what audio access is legal (01), what capture friction is achievable (02), and what Snipd and the
incumbents already occupy (03, 05).

Decide **which single concept variant, if any, is worth costing** — the input to the final verdict.

Bring to the conversation:

- The surviving capture mechanisms from 01, and what each costs the concept. If full audio clipping
  from Spotify is closed, the "connects to your existing podcast accounts" premise is dead and the
  app becomes a *player* the user must switch to — a far harder sell that must be confronted, not
  glossed.
- The best capture surface from 02, with its honest friction count. If the real answer is "two taps
  on the lock screen", say so — the concept claims near-zero friction and that claim should be
  held to account.
- The specific gap in Snipd, if 03 found one, and whether that gap is a *product* gap or a
  *nobody-wants-this* signal.

Questions to resolve with the developer:

1. Is a **player-replacement** app acceptable, given it requires abandoning their existing player?
   Or is the concept only interesting as a layer on top of existing listening?
2. If audio clipping is closed and only **transcript capture** survives, is that still the product
   they want to build — or was the audio clip the point?
3. Which segment from 04 are they actually building for, and do they belong to it themselves?
   Building for a segment you're not in, with no audience in it, is the hard mode of hard mode.
4. What's their **alternative use of this time**? Named as a gap in the map. A "don't build" verdict
   is meaningless without it, and it may turn out the real question is "is this the best of my
   ideas" rather than "is this viable".

Output: one named variant to cost, or the conclusion that no variant clears the bar — in which case
07's arithmetic becomes moot and the map short-circuits to the verdict.
